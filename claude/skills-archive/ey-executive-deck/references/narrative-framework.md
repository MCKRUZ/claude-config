# Narrative Frameworks for Executive Presentations

Choose the framework that matches your situation. Each framework creates a different emotional arc.

---

## Situation-Complication-Resolution (SCR)

**Best for:** Recommending a new initiative, proposing change

**The arc:** Establish stability → Introduce tension → Provide relief

### Structure

1. **Situation** (1-2 slides)
   - Where are we today?
   - What's the current state that everyone agrees on?
   - Establish common ground before introducing conflict

2. **Complication** (2-3 slides)
   - What has changed or will change?
   - Why can't we continue as we are?
   - Create urgency without panic

3. **Resolution** (3-4 slides)
   - What do we recommend?
   - Why will this work?
   - What evidence supports this?

### Example: Cloud Migration Proposal

| Slide | Type | Headline |
|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | Title | Cloud Migration Strategy for Enterprise Operations |
| 2 | Exec Summary | Migrating to Azure Reduces Costs 30% While Enabling AI Capabilities |
| 3 | Situation | Our Current Infrastructure Serves 2M Users Reliably |
| 4 | Complication | Legacy Systems Cannot Support Our AI Ambitions |
| 5 | Complication | Competitors Are Moving Faster—Acme Launched AI Features in Q2 |
| 6 | Resolution | A Phased Azure Migration Balances Speed and Risk |
| 7 | Evidence | Similar Migrations at Fortune 500s Achieved 28-35% Cost Reduction |
| 8 | Timeline | 18-Month Roadmap with Clear Milestones |
| 9 | Ask | Approve $4.2M Investment and Executive Sponsor Assignment |

## MECE Options with Recommendation

**Best for:** Presenting choices, strategic decisions with tradeoffs

**The arc:** Define the choice → Present options fairly → Advocate clearly

### Structure

1. **Decision Context** (1 slide)
   - What decision needs to be made?
   - What criteria matter most?

2. **Options Overview** (1 slide)
   - Present 2-4 mutually exclusive options
   - Show them as legitimate alternatives (not straw men)

3. **Option Deep-Dives** (1 slide each)
   - Pros, cons, and implications for each
   - Use consistent criteria across all options

4. **Recommendation** (1-2 slides)
   - Which option and why
   - Acknowledge tradeoffs directly

### Example: Market Entry Strategy

| Slide | Type | Headline |
|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | Title | European Market Entry Strategy |
| 2 | Exec Summary | Direct Entry via UK Acquisition Offers Best Risk-Adjusted Returns |
| 3 | Context | We Must Choose an Entry Mode by Q3 to Hit 2026 Targets |
| 4 | Options | Three Viable Paths: Acquisition, Partnership, or Organic Build |
| 5 | Option A | Acquisition Offers Speed but Requires $50M+ and Integration Risk |
| 6 | Option B | Partnership Reduces Risk but Limits Control and Margin |
| 7 | Option C | Organic Build Preserves Control but Takes 3+ Years |
| 8 | Comparison | Head-to-Head Analysis Favors Acquisition on 4 of 6 Criteria |
| 9 | Recommendation | Acquire TechCorp UK for Immediate Market Access |
| 10 | Ask | Authorize Due Diligence and $500K Advisory Engagement |

## Problem-Cause-Solution

**Best for:** Responding to issues, crisis management, fixing things

**The arc:** Acknowledge pain → Explain root cause → Show the fix

### Structure

1. **Problem Statement** (1-2 slides)
   - What's broken? What's the impact?
   - Quantify the pain

2. **Root Cause Analysis** (2-3 slides)
   - Why did this happen?
   - What systemic issues exist?
   - Resist blame; focus on systems

3. **Solution** (2-3 slides)
   - What will fix it?
   - How do we prevent recurrence?
   - What resources are needed?

### Example: Customer Churn Response

| Slide | Type | Headline |
|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | Title | Addressing Q3 Customer Churn |
| 2 | Exec Summary | Process Gaps Caused 23% Churn—Three Fixes Reverse the Trend |
| 3 | Problem | Enterprise Churn Hit 23% in Q3, Up from 8% Baseline |
| 4 | Problem | Churn Concentrated in First-Year Customers (37% vs. 11%) |
| 5 | Root Cause | Onboarding Takes 47 Days—3x Industry Average |
| 6 | Root Cause | Success Team Has 1:180 Ratio vs. Industry 1:80 |
| 7 | Solution | Automated Onboarding Cuts Time to Value by 60% |
| 8 | Solution | Adding 4 CSMs Brings Ratio to 1:95 |
| 9 | Timeline | 90-Day Implementation Plan |
| 10 | Ask | Approve $1.2M Investment in Platform and Headcount |

## Why-What-How-What If

**Best for:** Persuading skeptical audiences, change management

**The arc:** Create motivation → Define the vision → Show the path → Paint success

### Structure

1. **Why** (2-3 slides)
   - Why must we change?
   - What's at stake if we don't?
   - Build emotional urgency

2. **What** (2 slides)
   - What exactly are we proposing?
   - What does success look like?

3. **How** (2-3 slides)
   - How will we get there?
   - What's the roadmap?
   - Who is responsible?

4. **What If** (1-2 slides)
   - What if we succeed? Paint the vision.
   - What if we don't act? Show the risk.

### Example: Digital Transformation

| Slide | Type | Headline |
|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | Title | Digital Transformation Imperative |
| 2 | Exec Summary | Transform Now or Face 40% Revenue Decline by 2028 |
| 3 | Why | Customer Expectations Have Fundamentally Shifted |
| 4 | Why | Competitors Are Winning with Digital-First Models |
| 5 | Why | Our Current Systems Cannot Scale to Meet Demand |
| 6 | What | A Unified Platform Enables Seamless Customer Experience |
| 7 | What | Three Pillars: Data, Automation, AI |
| 8 | How | 24-Month Phased Approach Minimizes Disruption |
| 9 | How | Cross-Functional Teams Drive Each Workstream |
| 10 | What If | Success Means $200M New Revenue and Market Leadership |
| 11 | What If | Inaction Means Irrelevance Within 5 Years |
| 12 | Ask | Endorse the Strategy and Appoint Transformation Lead |

## Crafting Headlines That Work

Headlines are the most important text on every slide. They should:

### Assert, Don't Label
- ❌ "Market Analysis"
- ✅ "Market Share Declined 8% as Competitors Gained Ground"

### Be Specific
- ❌ "Sales Improved"
- ✅ "Sales Rose 24% YoY, Led by Enterprise Segment"

### Lead with the Insight
- ❌ "Survey Results Show Customer Satisfaction"
- ✅ "87% of Customers Recommend Us—Up from 72%"

### Use Active Voice
- ❌ "Cost Reductions Were Achieved"
- ✅ "Operations Cut Costs by $3.2M Through Automation"

### Test: If Headlines Only
Read just the headlines from slide 2 onward. They should tell the complete story. If someone reads only headlines and understands your recommendation, you've succeeded.
