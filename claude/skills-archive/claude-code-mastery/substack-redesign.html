Typography Strategy
Match your main site's type system within Substack's constraints

Your Main Site Fonts

Playfair Display
Headings, titles, logo — elegant serif
Source Sans 3 — Body text, navigation, labels. Clean, readable sans-serif with excellent legibility at all sizes.

Substack Font Selection

Substack won't have Playfair Display. In their font picker, choose:

  1. Titles: Pick the most elegant/decorative serif available. Spectral (your current choice) is actually reasonable for titles on dark backgrounds.
  2. Body: Pick a clean sans-serif option if available, or keep the serif but ensure line spacing is comfortable.
  3. Workaround: Use a custom wordmark image rendered in Playfair Display to get exact font matching on the header (see Logo section).

Anti-Patterns
What to avoid on dark Substack themes

Dark Theme Pitfalls

  • X Pure white (#fff) text on pure black (#000) — too harsh. Use #fafafa on #0a0a0b.
  • X Bright accent glow/neon effects — gold should be warm, not electric
  • X Colorful post covers — they'll clash. Keep covers dark + subtle accent
  • X Emojis in titles on dark backgrounds — they break the premium feel
  • X Inconsistent cover art styles — use the template system above

Contrast Checks (WCAG)

  • #fafafa on #0a0a0b — 19.4:1 ratio (exceeds AAA)
  • #d4a853 on #0a0a0b — 7.8:1 ratio (exceeds AAA)
  • #a1a1aa on #0a0a0b — 7.2:1 ratio (exceeds AAA)
  • #71717a on #0a0a0b — 4.6:1 ratio (passes AA)
